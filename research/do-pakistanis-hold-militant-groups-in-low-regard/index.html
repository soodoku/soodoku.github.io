Which Pakistanis Hold Militant Groups in “Low Regard”? Three Interviewers in Blair et al.’s Endorsement Experiment
🗃Code and Data
Three of the 75 interviewers in a Pakistan survey recorded the same four policy answers in most of their interviews. Their 313 respondents came almost entirely from middle- and high-income households. Excluding their interviews reduces the estimated difference between poor and middle-class respondents from 2.2 to 1.0 points on a 100-point scale. The remaining data are consistent with both zero and the published estimate. The average penalty for a militant group’s endorsement grows from 1.3 to 2.1 points, but that average says little about how many people favored the endorsement.
