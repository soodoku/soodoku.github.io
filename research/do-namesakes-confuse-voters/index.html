Do Namesakes Confuse Voters? Evidence from Six Decades of Indian Elections Draft
🗃Replication MaterialsRelated: Indian election results, 2023–2026, in TCPD's layout
Indian ballots often list minor independents who share a leading candidate's name. Across 570,241 Lok Sabha and assembly candidate records from 1962 to 2026, independents named like a leading candidate place 7.8 percentile points higher among their contest's independents than other independents (SE 1.4), while those named like a minor-party candidate gain nothing detectable. The votes involved are few: counting only votes attributable to the name, namesakes exceeded the winning margin in 12 of 57,166 contests (95% range 4–18). The premium shrank after candidate photographs were added to voting machines in 2015, but placebo break dates produce declines of the same size.
