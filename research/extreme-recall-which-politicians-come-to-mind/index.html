Extreme Recall: Which Politicians Come to Mind?
With Daniel Weitzel.
Journal of Elections, Public Opinion and Parties. 2024.
🗃Replication MaterialsRelated: Extreme Recall
Two surveys nearly a decade apart show that people's images of political parties center on a few prominent national figures, with more extreme politicians recalled more often. Television news coverage is associated with which politicians come to mind.
