Holier Than Thou? No Large Partisan Gap in Consumption of Pornography Online
With Lucas Shen.
Journal of Quantitative Description: Digital Media, 4, 2024.
🗃Replication Materials
Observed browsing data show that online pornography consumption is concentrated among a small share of users. Republicans consume somewhat more than Democrats in the unadjusted data, but the difference disappears after accounting for age and gender.
