Propagation of Error: Citations to Retracted Research
With Ken Cor.
🗃Replication Materials
We compare each of 8,627 retracted research articles, published 2000–2019 and retracted 2005–2022, with up to five articles from the same journal and publication year matched on pre-retraction citations, using OpenAlex citation histories through 2025 for both. In the three complete years after the retraction year, citations to retracted articles were 62% lower than the path of their comparison articles implies, and the gap keeps widening for a decade. Retracted articles nonetheless continue to be cited: across 8,963 retracted articles, 27% of all citations arrived after the retraction year, five years on 39% of the articles observed that long were still being cited, and in a hand-coded sample 91% of post-retraction citations noted no concern with the cited article.
