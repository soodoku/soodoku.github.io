Women and Men on Delhi’s Streets: Evidence from Field Photographs, 2016–2017
Reviewers counted people in 1,958 street photographs taken at 196 Delhi locations between November 2016 and January 2017. Women accounted for 18.5% of sightings across the photographs. Field collection departed from the planned random sample, limiting what the photographs can establish about Delhi as a whole.
