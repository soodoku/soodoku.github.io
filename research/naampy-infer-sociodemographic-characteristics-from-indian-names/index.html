📦naampy: Infer Sociodemographic Characteristics from Indian Names
With Rajashekar Chintalapati and Suriyan Laohaprapanon.Related: 📦outkast: caste-name data; Indian electoral rolls
naampy estimates aggregate patterns associated with Indian first names using calibrated model scores or exact source-data lookups. Results include abstention and artifact provenance; the scores describe patterns in recorded labels, not an individual's gender.
