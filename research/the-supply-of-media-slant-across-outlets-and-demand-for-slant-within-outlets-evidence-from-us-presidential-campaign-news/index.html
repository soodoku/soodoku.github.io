The Supply of Media Slant Across Outlets and Demand for Slant Within Outlets: Evidence from US Presidential Campaign News
With Marcel Garz, Daniel Stone, and Justin Wallace.
European Journal of Political Economy, 63, 101877, 2020.
🗃Replication Materials
We study presidential campaign horse-race headlines across six online news outlets in 2012 and 2016. Headlines tend to favor their outlets' typical readers, but within-outlet engagement provides little evidence that readers prefer congenial headlines and somewhat more evidence of demand for uncongenial ones.
