Revisiting a Natural Experiment: Do Legislators With Daughters Vote More Liberally on Women's Issues?
With Don Green, Oliver Hyman-Metzger, and Michelle Zee.
Journal of Political Economy Microeconomics. 2023.
🗃Replication Materials | Supporting InformationPress: Phys.org
We revisit evidence that legislators with daughters vote more liberally on women's issues, extending the analysis to eight congresses before and eight after the original study period. We find no daughters effect in either extension, challenging the explanation that later party polarization alone erased the relationship.
