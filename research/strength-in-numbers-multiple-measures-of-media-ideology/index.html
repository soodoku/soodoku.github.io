Strength in Numbers: Multiple Measures of Media Ideology
With Philip Habel.
🗃Replication Materials
We combine text-based and audience-based measures of British news outlets' ideology. Parliamentary speeches, party manifestos, and Twitter following patterns provide complementary information about ideological position and differences between measurement approaches.
