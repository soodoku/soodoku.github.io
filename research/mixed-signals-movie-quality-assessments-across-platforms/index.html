Mixed Signals: Movie Quality Assessments Across Platforms
We compare movie ratings across platforms using data on 16,319 American films released between 1950 and 2020. Among platforms with sufficient coverage, average ratings are only moderately correlated, revealing substantial differences in assessments of the same films.
