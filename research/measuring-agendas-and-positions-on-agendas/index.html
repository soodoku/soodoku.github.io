Measuring Agendas and Positions on Agendas
With Andrew Guess.
🗃Replication Materials
We distinguish which issues news outlets cover from the positions they take within those issues. A supervised topic model and congressional-speech-based slant measures reveal systematic differences across 255 news programs on 50 television channels.
