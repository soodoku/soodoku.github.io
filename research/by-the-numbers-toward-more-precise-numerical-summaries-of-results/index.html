By the Numbers: Toward More Precise Numerical Summaries of Results
With Andrew Guess.
The Political Methodologist. 24(1): 2016.
🗃Replication Materials
We compare how often empirical articles in the American Political Science Review and American Economic Review include precise numerical results in their abstracts. The project provides coded data and analysis of differences in quantitative reporting.
