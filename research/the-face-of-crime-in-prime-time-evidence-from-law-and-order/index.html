The Face of Crime in Prime Time: Evidence from Law and Order
With Daniel Trielli.
🗃Replication MaterialsPress: The Washington Post
We tally the coded race and gender of victims and criminals across three Law and Order series. Black characters appear at lower shares than in several historical arrest and victimization series, while women appear at higher shares among criminals than in several arrest categories. The comparisons are descriptive because the sources differ in crimes, places, and years.
