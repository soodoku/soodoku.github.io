Is Cynicism Taken as Evidence of Political Sophistication?
With Alexander G. Theodoridis.
🗃Replication Materials
In a two-arm comparison in the 2020 Cooperative Election Study, 711 respondents rated one of two tweets. Both said the system rewards bad behavior; one said politics attracts good people, the other bad people. The bad-people tweet scored 6.2 points lower in perceived sophistication on a 0–100 scale (95% CI: −9.4 to −3.0). Covariate adjustment and survey weighting preserve the negative difference. A causal interpretation requires random assignment within the rating sample; the original questionnaire and randomization program are unavailable.
