Hard News: The Softening of Network Television News
With Daniel Weitzel.
🗃Replication Materials
A coded sample of more than 5,000 network television news segments from 1968 to 2019 documents changes in political content and geographic focus. Coverage unrelated to politics increases over time, and local news becomes a substantially larger share in the later years.
