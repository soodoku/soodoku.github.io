The Hostile Audience: The Effect of Access to Broadband Internet on Partisan Affect
With Yphtach Lelkes and Shanto Iyengar.
American Journal of Political Science. 61(1): 5–20, 2017.
🗃Replication Materials · Published VersionPress: The Guardian
We use variation in state right-of-way regulations to study the effect of broadband access on partisan hostility. Combining broadband availability with survey data from 2004 and 2008, we find greater hostility and greater consumption of partisan media where broadband access expands.
