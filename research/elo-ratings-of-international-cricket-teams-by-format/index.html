Elo Ratings of International Cricket Teams By Format
With Derek Willis.Press: The Hindu
We construct historical monthly Elo ratings for international cricket teams, separately for Tests, one-day internationals, and T20 internationals. The repository provides match outcomes, rating scripts, and downloadable series for comparing team strength over time.
