Methodological Criticism and Continued Citation
With Ken Cor.
🗃Replication Materials
Finding an error can improve later research only if researchers respond to it. We examine citations after methodological audits in neuroscience, animal experiments, and political science. OpenAlex histories through 2025 show continued citation to flagged neuroscience papers: their median annual citations rose from 6.5 in 2010 to 14–19 during 2012–2015 and were 7 in 2025. Comparisons within the same journal and publication year suggest somewhat slower proportional citation growth after the critique. A separate sample of citing passages rarely acknowledged a concern. The paper distinguishes persistent attention from the effect of publicity on citation growth.
