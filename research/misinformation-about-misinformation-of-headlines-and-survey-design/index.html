Misinformation About Misinformation? Of Headlines and Survey Design
With Robert C. Luskin, Yul Min Park, and Joshua Blank.
🗃Replication Materials
Media-poll misinformation items rarely offer a 'don't know' and usually ask what respondents think. In a survey experiment, offering and encouraging 'don't know' cut incorrect answers from 32% to 20%, repeating the false claim in the question changed nothing, and asking how definitely true or false each statement is cut them to 7%.

Cite: Gaurav Sood, Robert C. Luskin, Yul Min Park, and Joshua Blank. "Misinformation About Misinformation? Of Headlines and Survey Design." https://gsood.com/research/misinformation-about-misinformation-of-headlines-and-survey-design/
