📦Notnews: Predict the Type of News Based on Story Text and URL
With Suriyan Laohaprapanon.
A Python library for classifying news by topic and distinguishing hard from soft news. It offers URL-pattern rules, trained US and UK text models, and optional language-model classification through Python and command-line interfaces.
