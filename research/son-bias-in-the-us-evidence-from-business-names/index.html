Son Bias in the US: Evidence from Business Names
With Walter Guillioli.
🗃Replication Materials
We compare businesses named with 'son' or 'sons' and 'daughter' or 'daughters' using state business registries. Across 40 states, the median ratio is 12 to 1, with limitations in registry searches making the analysis a conservative accounting of the imbalance.
