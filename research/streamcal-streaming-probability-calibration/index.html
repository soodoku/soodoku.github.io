📦streamcal: Streaming Probability Calibration
streamcal keeps a deployed model's binary probabilities calibrated as outcomes arrive, without retaining its prediction history. It fits a monotone (isotonic) correction from fixed-size per-bin sufficient statistics, can down-weight stale history, and predicts before observing each outcome so a forecast never uses its own label.
