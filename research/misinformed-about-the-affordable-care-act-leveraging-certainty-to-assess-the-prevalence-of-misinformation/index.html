Misinformed About the Affordable Care Act? Leveraging Certainty to Assess the Prevalence of Misinformation
With Josh Pasek and Jon Krosnick.
Journal of Communication. 65(4): 660–673, 2015.
🗃Supporting Information | 🗃Replication Materials
Two national surveys distinguish incorrect answers about the Affordable Care Act from confidently held incorrect beliefs. Most respondents are uncertain; confident misperceptions are less common and are concentrated on provisions about which elites repeatedly made false claims.
