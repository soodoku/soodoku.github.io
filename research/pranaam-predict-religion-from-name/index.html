📦pranaam: Predict Religion From Name
With Rajashekar Chintalapati.
pranaam estimates how strongly a name follows patterns associated with Muslim names in its training data. It returns a calibrated score, abstention information, and artifact provenance for aggregate research, rather than establishing a person's religion.
