Predicting Race and Ethnicity From the Sequence of Characters in a Name 🌱
With Rajashekar Chintalapati and Suriyan Laohaprapanon.
arXiv:1805.02109, first posted in 2018 and revised in 2023.
📦ethnicolr Python packagePress: InfoQ; AnacondaCON presentation (Video)
The authors compare models that use the letters in a name to estimate recorded race and ethnicity. Their best model classifies 85% of held-out cases correctly using full names. With surnames alone, the best model classifies 81% correctly.

Cite: Rajashekar Chintalapati, Suriyan Laohaprapanon, and Gaurav Sood (2023). "Predicting Race and Ethnicity From the Sequence of Characters in a Name." arXiv:1805.02109, first posted in 2018 and revised in 2023. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1805.02109
