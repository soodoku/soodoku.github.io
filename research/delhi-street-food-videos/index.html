Who Eats in Delhi Street-Food Videos? 🌱
In 450 randomly sampled frames from 30 Delhi street-food videos published in 2025, women accounted for 13.5% of apparent diner sightings and 16.1% of all sightings with classifiable gender, after weighting each video equally. A person with a serving of food in hand or in front of them counts as a diner, as does someone sharing a meal.

Cite: Gaurav Sood. "Who Eats in Delhi Street-Food Videos?." https://gsood.com/research/delhi-street-food-videos/
