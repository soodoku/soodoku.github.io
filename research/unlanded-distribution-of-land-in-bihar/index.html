Unlanded: Distribution of Land in Bihar
With Lucas Shen.
🗃Replication Materials
Digitized land records from Bihar reveal concentrated ownership and substantial disparities by gender, religion, and caste. Men make up 76.5% of landowners, and Muslims, Scheduled Castes, and Extremely Backward Classes are underrepresented; the study measures ownership rather than operational agricultural holdings.
