Measuring Perceptions of Numerical Strength of Salient and Stereotypical Groups
With Doug Ahler.
Misinformation and Mass Audiences. 2018. University of Texas Press.
🗃Replication Materials | Appendix
This chapter examines how to measure beliefs about the numerical size of salient and stereotypical groups. It connects systematic overestimation of stereotypical traits to broader questions about group perceptions, prejudice, and the interpretation of survey estimates.
