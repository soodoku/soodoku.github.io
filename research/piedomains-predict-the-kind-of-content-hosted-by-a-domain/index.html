📦piedomains: Predict the Kind of Content Hosted by a Domain
With Rajashekar Chintalapati.Related: Domain Knowledge: Predicting the Kind of Content Hosted by a Domain.
With Suriyan Laohaprapanon. Complex, Intelligent and Software Intensive Systems (CISIS), 2020.; 📦rdomains: domain classification in R
The package infers the kind of content hosted by a domain using the domain name, the textual content, and the screenshot of the homepage. We use domain category labels from Shallalist and build our own training dataset by scraping and taking screenshots of the homepage.

Cite: Gaurav Sood and Rajashekar Chintalapati. "piedomains: Predict the Kind of Content Hosted by a Domain." https://gsood.com/research/piedomains-predict-the-kind-of-content-hosted-by-a-domain/
