🌱 Women and Men on Urban Streets: Evidence from Crowdsourced Photographs in 101 Cities
🗃Replication Materials
Across 28,794 ground-level photographs from 101 cities, women accounted for 36.0% of classified pedestrian sightings. The share ranged from 16.0% in the Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan, and Pakistan to 49.4% in Europe and Central Asia. The paper compares the photographs with human counts and local population records.

Cite: Gaurav Sood. "Women and Men on Urban Streets: Evidence from Crowdsourced Photographs in 101 Cities." https://gsood.com/research/missing-women-on-global-streets/
