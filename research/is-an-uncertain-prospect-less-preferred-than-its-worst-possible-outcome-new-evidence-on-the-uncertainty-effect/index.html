Is an Uncertain Prospect Less Preferred Than Its Worst Possible Outcome? New Evidence on the Uncertainty Effect
With Doug Ahler.
🗃Replication Materials
Two larger surveys replicate the finding that people sometimes prefer a certain option to a lottery whose worst outcome is better. Clarifying the guarantee reduces the effect, while expressing the lottery as natural frequencies or using monetary choices eliminates it in these experiments.
