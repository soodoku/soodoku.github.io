📊Indian Electoral Rolls: Data and Extraction Tools
With Atul Dhingra.Related: Searchable-roll parser; Scanned-roll parser
Scripts and linked tools collect and parse Indian electoral rolls published as PDFs by state election authorities. The project provides a foundation for research using recorded names, ages, gender labels, family relationships, and electoral geography.

Cite: Gaurav Sood and Atul Dhingra. "Indian Electoral Rolls: Data and Extraction Tools." https://gsood.com/research/indian-electoral-rolls/
