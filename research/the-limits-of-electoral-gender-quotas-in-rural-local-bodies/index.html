The Limits of Electoral Gender Quotas in Rural Local Bodies
With Varun Karekurve-Ramachandra.
🗃Replication MaterialsRelated: Governance; Public spending; PDS; MarriagePress: VoxDev
Across more than 34,000 rural local bodies, prior reservation produces small or inconsistent short-run gains in women's success in subsequently open seats. Phone audits document extensive male mediation of access to women elected under quotas, a pattern consistent with, but not proof of, proxy governance limiting independent political careers.
