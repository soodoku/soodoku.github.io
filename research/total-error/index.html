One Label, Many Records: Shared Prediction Error in Regression
🗃Replication MaterialsRelated: Unbiased Regression with Costly Item Labels
One predicted item label can enter many records and several constructed variables. This paper traces the consequences for coefficients and standard errors when aggregates are regression outcomes, covariates, or both. It connects generated-variable and shared-source inference, gives joint moment corrections and covariance estimates under item validation, and states the additional conditions required for normal coefficient intervals. Simulations distinguish coefficient bias, empirical variability, estimated standard errors, and coverage, including failures with influential or few items. Browsing-data illustrations show the roles of reuse, visit concentration, and observed classifier disagreement.
