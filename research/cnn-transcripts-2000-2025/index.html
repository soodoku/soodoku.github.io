💾CNN Transcripts 2000--2025Related: Internet Archive TV captions; Vanderbilt TV news abstracts; NBC / MSNBC transcripts
A corpus and scraper for CNN transcripts spanning 2000 through March 2025, with program names, dates, times, and transcript text. The repository documents collection across versions of CNN's site and links to research-access data on Harvard Dataverse.
