The Older Half: Spousal Age Gap in India
With Suriyan Laohaprapanon.Related: Indian electoral rolls
Using electoral-roll records for nearly 70 million couples across Indian states and union territories, we describe spousal age differences. Husbands are generally older, with an average gap of about 4.1 years and substantial variation across states and spouses' ages.
