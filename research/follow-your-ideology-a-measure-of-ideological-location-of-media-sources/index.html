Follow Your Ideology: Measuring Media Ideology on Social Networks
With Pablo Barberá.
We estimate the ideological positions of more than 2,300 media sources using the following patterns of politically interested social-media users. Comparisons with content-based measures support the approach, and applications examine journalists' ideology and ideological diversity within outlets.
